# Wilderness Loadouts

Wilderness Loadouts helps you prepare for Wilderness trips by finding the strongest defensive setup from equipment you own, based on how much you are willing to risk.

It is designed for activities such as Wilderness Agility, Rogues' Castle chests, clue hunting, and other trips where survivability and replacement cost matter.

## Features

- Recommends gear from your owned equipment
- Optimizes for Magic, Overall, Ranged, or Melee Defence
- Accounts for your risk limit and protected items
- Lets you override recommendations and lock specific items
- Shows owned alternatives for each equipment slot
- Creates an optional Bank Tag layout for the recommended loadout

## Getting started

Open your bank so the plugin can find your equipment, then open the **Wilderness Loadouts** sidebar. Choose your defensive focus, protected-item count, and risk limit. The recommended loadout updates automatically.

Risk values are planning estimates. Always check the in-game **Items Kept on Death** interface before entering the Wilderness.
